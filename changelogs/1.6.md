# 1.6.0
#### Native Camera API
- Added `Player::getCamera()` for presets, camera instructions, fades, shakes, FOV, targeting, and entity attachment.
- Added a protocol-aware camera preset registry and builder for custom presets.

#### Education content
- Added `education.enabled` to `pocketmine.yml` to hide built-in Education items from the creative inventory and bundled legacy chemistry resource packs.
- Enabled by default; existing chemistry blocks, items, and recipes remain available.
